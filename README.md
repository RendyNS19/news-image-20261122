# The Daily Ledger — Sunday, November 22, 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Sunday,
22 November 2026, drawn with Python PIL (`draw.py`, data in `stories.json`)
and shipped as `entry.png` (1024×1024).

**Hero:** COP31 collapses in Antalya — the EU and island nations reject a
draft with no mention of fossil fuels; the longest climate summit on record
ends with no overarching agreement.

**Tiles:** a Russian corvette sunk by suspected Ukrainian sea drones off
Benghazi · a Russian drone flattens an apartment block in Dymer, near Kyiv ·
Iran's strike wounds two in Bahrain · cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" captured
in Sinaloa · BepiColombo reaches Mercury after an eight-year journey.

**Wide tile:** Bitcoin tops $61,000 in a weekend rebound from Saturday's
two-year low near $55,100.

**In brief:** Indonesia's candy-factory fire · West Bank settler attacks ·
Denmark expels a Russian attaché · NATO council meets Monday · FBI database
hack · UK–EU deal.

**Markets strip:** Friday 20 November closes, labelled as such — markets
were shut Saturday and Sunday.
